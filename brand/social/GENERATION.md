## Final profile prompt

Use case: logo-brand.
Create one finished, original logo image for SETSUNA, an onchain finance app on Monad. This is the X profile avatar asset, not a presentation board. Square 1024 x 1024 raster image.
Brand direction from the actual app: Japanese hanko seal, the character 刹 (first character in 刹那), the idea of an instant. Colors: near-black #0b0b0c, vivid vermilion #ff5a36, warm ivory #f2efe9.
Design: a single bold vermilion square seal centered on a completely flat near-black square canvas. The seal is about 64 percent of the canvas width, very slightly rounded corners, optically centered, generous clean negative space so the complete mark fits inside a circular avatar crop. Inside the seal render ONE large, exact and legible Japanese kanji 刹 in warm ivory. Use a distinctive architectural, angular, bold seal-cut treatment with clear separated strokes, preserving the correct character and making it readable at small sizes. The character must be 刹, not another kanji or invented pseudo-lettering. Crisp, carefully balanced solid forms, confident silhouette, meticulous negative space. Premium contemporary Japanese editorial identity.
Entire artwork flat and graphic, two solid colors against the dark background; no lighting, no shadows, no grain, no gradients, no bevel, no 3D, no metallic surface, no mockup, no border around the canvas. No brand wordmark, no English letters, no subtitles, no watermarks, no extra symbols. Produce a single ready-to-use square avatar image.

## Final cover prompt

Use case: ads-marketing.
Create a finished X / Twitter profile HEADER COVER for SETSUNA, an onchain spot trading, perpetuals and automated Earn app being built on Monad.
Input image 1 is a BRAND LOGO REFERENCE only: preserve the identity of the vermilion square seal with the exact ivory character 刹. Do not reproduce the reference's square canvas as the output.
OUTPUT MUST BE AN ULTRAWIDE 3:1 BANNER, target 1500 x 500 pixels, or a larger exact 3:1 native canvas. One complete horizontal cover, no surrounding mockup, no presentation board.
Art direction: striking, premium Japanese editorial finance identity. Matte near-black #0b0b0c background, warm ivory #f2efe9 typography, vivid vermilion #ff5a36 accent. Extremely subtle tactile paper grain in the banner background, almost invisible. Rich restrained contrast, precise and spacious, confident typography.
Composition tailored for X: keep the lower-left quarter mostly dark and empty for the overlapping circular profile avatar. Put the main typography in the center-left, starting around 25% of canvas width. Keep all text comfortably in the middle vertical band, away from top and bottom crop edges. Big elegant editorial serif wordmark in ivory, exact text SETSUNA, beautifully kerned, approximately 9% of the full canvas height per capital? Instead make the wordmark genuinely prominent at approximately 23% of total canvas height, clearly the primary text. Under it a smaller clean sans-serif line reading exactly Spot. Perps. Earn. and a small widely spaced monospace line reading exactly ON MONAD.
At the right, around 80% of canvas width, a single refined sculptural red seal tile with the exact 刹 mark from reference, modest 3D depth, very slight oblique angle, lacquer-matte vermilion face, ivory graphic strokes, a subtle orange-red rim highlight. Keep the mark immediately recognizable as the avatar's same logo. Three very fine, fluid vermilion trajectories curve through the dark space behind the seal, suggesting capital moving through three products, mostly at the right edge. Restrained warm ambient glow behind the seal; don't let the decorative light trails touch the text.
Text (verbatim and only these three lines): SETSUNA / Spot. Perps. Earn. / ON MONAD.
No claimed returns, no percentages, no prices, no candlestick charts, no fake app screens, no coins, no stock imagery, no sponsor logos, no X logo, no web address, no watermark. A distinctive cohesive ready-to-upload cover that matches the reference profile logo.
