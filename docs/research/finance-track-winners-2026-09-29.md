## Interpretation for Setsuna (inference, not judges' stated reasons)

These winners cover different kinds of value: access to credit (Yumi), routing
and execution (Hop/Urani), asset management (GLAM/Magma), trading venues (Pismo/
Squeeze), privacy (Vanish/Blackpool), liquidity provision (MetEngine), and mobile
interfaces/distribution (PIPS/Yosuku).

The sample does not establish that feature count caused a win. Several products
are focused. A better benchmark is a clear user outcome and a complete workflow
that makes the outcome visible. Existing Setsuna code demonstrates a mechanism;
research has not established demand or willingness to pay for the standalone app.
These winner announcements do not establish those projects' PMF either.

Useful precedents to investigate before changing scope:
- GLAM: how a capital-management product structures user roles and permissions.
- MetEngine: how a focused liquidity-provision workflow becomes a product.
- PIPS/Yosuku: what makes the trading interaction and mobile distribution distinct.

No pivot or code change is selected by this research.
